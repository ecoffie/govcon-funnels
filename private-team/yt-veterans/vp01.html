SDVOSB moved VA → SBA · re-confirm before recording

Verified true (re-confirm on SBA site)

  • SDVOSB certification is now run by the SBA, not the VA — moved effective Jan 1, 2023
  • You apply at veterans.certify.sba.gov (VetCert) — the old VA VIP/VetBiz portal is retired
  • Certification is free — SBA does not charge a fee to apply; paid "we'll do it for you" services are optional, not required
  • Self-certification was phased out — to win SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contracts you must be SBA-certified
  • Set-aside means exclusive — a non-veteran firm cannot bid on an SDVOSB set-aside; you compete in a smaller pool
  • Core eligibility — service-connected disability rating from the VA, veteran must own 51%+ and control day-to-day + long-term decisions
  • You must be in SAM.gov with a UEI first — both free, done before/with the VetCert application
  • There is a government-wide SDVOSB contracting goal (3%) — the reason set-asides exist

Numbers held back on purpose

The playlist letter cites ~$32.8B set aside for service-disabled veterans — treat that as a verify-before-you-say-it figure. If you put a dollar number on screen, pull the current fiscal-year SDVOSB total from USASpending/SBA at film time; do not read last year's number over this year's footage.

The video's credibility rests on the process being right — which the SBA site confirms — not on a headline dollar figure. Get the portal and the steps correct and the number is optional b-roll.
