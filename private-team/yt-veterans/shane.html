The real story

Everything below is on the record · landmines flagged

Verified true (from tape)

  • Army veteran — IT/comms in Afghanistan; learned M&A out of the military at a firm valuing businesses
  • Co-founded Falcon Tech and Gov Brew (the social brand → awareness engine)
  • Spotted the baby-boomer succession wave → decided buying beat building
  • After ~7–8 months searching, bought an SDVOSB, ~20 employees, with active prime + subcontract vehicles already attached
  • Financed with an SBA loan as a veteran — First Citizens Bank (delegated, expedited), 3–5 month close, little out of pocket
  • Built the delivery team first → scaled as subcontractor → chased primes; used Gov Brew for relationships
  • Won a $10M VA BPA → $1M → $15M revenue, 20 → 225 employees
  • Lines to keep: "swing outside your weight class" · acquisition = instant credibility · "relationships matter more than strategy initially"

Landmines — do not overclaim

It's an SBA loan he accessed as a veteran, NOT a grant. The $10M was a VA BPA, not a random award.

⚠️ Shane says "SBA veterans advantage" on tape, but the old broad "SBA Veterans Advantage" 7(a) fee waiver is gone — do not imply a viewer gets that same fee waiver today. The current veteran loan benefit is the SBA Express zero-guaranty-fee (up to $500K). Describe Shane's financing generically ("an SBA loan, using his veteran status") and route viewers to Ep 04 for current programs.

SBA lending-threshold increase is real — frame as "current administration raised thresholds"; verify the number before quoting one.
