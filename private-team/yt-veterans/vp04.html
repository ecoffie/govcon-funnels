═══ COLD OPEN (grounded in the tape — David Pere) ═══

"The VA loan is the single best primary residence mortgage in the world. There's nothing like it."

That's David Pere — a Navy vet who built a real estate empire on it. So here's the part that should make you angry: he says only about 6% of veterans ever use it. And the ones who don't? A lender or an agent talked them out of it — because they don't understand the product, so they steer you into one they do.

It happened to David himself. First house, a so-called VA lender told him, "You can only use a VA loan once. Don't waste it on this duplex." David listened. He was very wrong — it cost him ten, fifteen grand and PMI he never should have paid.

Same story with disability. Vets who never file — "ego comes in. People are like, I'm not hurt. I'm good. I'm fine." Meanwhile a 10% rating waives the funding fee — twenty to forty thousand dollars on a house. Money you already earned, walking out the door.

═══ THE TURN (up-funnel seed — record or splice) ═══

And here's why this matters on this channel: the same government that guarantees that loan is legally required to buy from your business too. Same benefits. Bigger check. Stick around.

Facts checked

Guest-attributed · confirm David's exact numbers on tape

Directionally true (attribute to David / VA)

  • VA home loan = $0 down, no PMI — widely regarded as one of the strongest mortgage products; David's core claim
  • Most eligible veterans never use it — usage is a fraction of those entitled (David's "biggest lie" framing)
  • A disability rating can waive the VA funding fee — real money saved on a VA purchase
  • Many veterans avoid filing for disability — stigma / "someone needs it more" myth
  • The VA loan is reusable — entitlement can be restored / used more than once
  • GI Bill / education benefits cover trade schools, pay a housing stipend, and extend to dependents at 100% rating
  • Lenders and agents sometimes steer veterans away from VA loans (more paperwork on their end) — David's warning
