═══ COLD OPEN ═══

There is one customer on planet Earth that spent a hundred and eighty-three billion dollars with small businesses last year.

Not Amazon. Not Walmart. Not Apple.

And here's the strange part.

Almost nobody is trying to sell to them.

I'm going to show you who this customer is, exactly what they bought, and why the businesses that figure this out quietly get rich while everyone else fights over the same crowded markets.

Stay with me. Because by the end of this video you're going to look at your own business completely differently.


═══ WHO IT IS ═══

Okay. Let me just say it.

The biggest customer on earth is the United States government.

Now — I know. The second I say "government," half of you want to click away.

You're picturing paperwork. Red tape. Something complicated that isn't for you.

Hold on. Give me two minutes.

Because that reaction — that exact reaction — is the reason this opportunity is wide open.

Everybody assumes it's too hard. So they never look.

And that leaves the field almost empty for the people who do.


═══ THE NUMBER ═══

Here's the number that should stop you.

Last year, the federal government awarded over a hundred and eighty-three billion dollars in contracts specifically to small businesses.

Not giant corporations. Small businesses.

To put that in perspective —

the Department of Defense alone spent four hundred and forty-six billion dollars on contracts.

The Department of Veterans Affairs, sixty-seven billion.

Energy, forty-six billion.

Health and Human Services, thirty billion.

This is the largest, most reliable buyer in the history of the world.

And they are legally required to spend a chunk of it with small companies.


═══ THE MYTH ═══

Now let's kill the myth.

You think they're only buying fighter jets and rockets.

They're not.

Last year the government spent thirty-eight billion dollars just on facilities support — basically running and maintaining buildings.

Almost six billion on security guards.

Two billion on janitorial services. Cleaning.

Over a billion on food service. Feeding people.

Landscaping. Plumbing. Heating and air conditioning.

These are not high-tech businesses.

These are boring, ordinary, everyday businesses.

The same kind of business somebody in your town is running right now — except that person is billing the most reliable customer on earth, and their competitor down the street has no idea it's even possible.


═══ WHY NOBODY TALKS ABOUT IT ═══

So why doesn't anybody talk about this?

Three reasons.

Number one — it sounds boring. "Government contracting" is a phrase that makes people's eyes glaze over. Nobody's making it exciting. So it stays invisible.

Number two — the people who ARE doing it have zero incentive to tell you. Why would they? Less competition for them.

And number three — everyone assumes it's harder than it is. They picture a wall of forms. But the front door is simpler than you think, and I'll point you to it in a second.

That's the whole game. It's hidden in plain sight. A hundred and eighty-three billion dollars, sitting right there, because the branding is bad and nobody bothered to look.


═══ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU ═══

So what does this actually mean for you?

It means the customer you've been ignoring might be the biggest one you'll ever have.

It means you don't need a new business.

You need a new buyer for the business you already have.

The cleaning company. The IT service. The staffing agency. The construction crew. The consultant.

Whatever you do — there is a real chance the government is already buying it from somebody.

The only question is whether it's you.


═══ THE TURN — HOW YOU'D FIND IT ═══

And that's the part people get stuck on.

"Okay Eric, that sounds great — but how do I actually see what they're buying? Where do I even look?"

Fair question.

All of this spending is public. Every contract, every dollar, every agency — it's all out in the open, by law. The problem is knowing where to look and what to actually do first.

So I put the whole starting playbook into a free guide. How to get in the system, how to find what they're buying in your industry, and the exact first moves — no fluff, no cost.

It's completely free. The link's in the description. Grab it, and you'll have the real roadmap in front of you tonight.


═══ CLOSE ═══

Look — I've watched ordinary people build serious businesses on this one idea.

Not because they were smarter. Not because they had connections.

Just because they looked where nobody else was looking.

The biggest customer on earth is open for business.

A hundred and eighty-three billion dollars a year, and most people will never even glance at it.

Don't be most people.

Grab the free guide in the description and take the first step tonight.

And subscribe, because I'm doing a whole series on winning this the real way, step by step.

I'll see you in the next one.
