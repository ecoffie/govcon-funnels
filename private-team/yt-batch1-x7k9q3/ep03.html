═══ COLD OPEN ═══

There's one customer on earth that's legally required to buy from small businesses — can't ghost you, can't stiff you. Last year it spent a hundred eighty-three billion dollars with companies like yours. Here's who it is and how to sell to them.

Stay with me. Because by the end of this video, you're going to look at your own business completely differently.


═══ THE WEDGE ═══

Here's something nobody says out loud.

Every other creator online is teaching you how to MAKE money. Build the offer. Run the ads. Chase the lead. Convince the customer. Hope they don't ghost you. Hope the card doesn't decline.

Nobody hands you the customer.

I'm going to hand you the customer.

And it's not a "hack." It's the largest, most reliable buyer in the history of the world — and it is legally obligated to spend a piece of its budget with small companies like yours every single year.


═══ WHO IT IS ═══

Okay. Let me just say it.

The biggest customer on earth is the United States government.

Now — I know. The second I say "government," half of you want to click away. You're picturing paperwork. Red tape. Something complicated that isn't for you.

Hold on. Give me two minutes.

Because that reaction — that exact reaction — is the reason this opportunity is wide open. Everybody assumes it's too hard. So they never look. And that leaves the field almost empty for the people who do.

Here's the part that changes everything. This customer is not like any customer you've ever had.

It can't ghost you. When it says it's buying, it publishes exactly what it's buying, out in the open, by law.

It can't stiff you. When it signs a contract, it pays. Not "maybe." Not "when the client feels like it." It pays — because it's the federal government.

Is it slower? Yes. Is there more paperwork? Yes. That's the trade. Slower and more paperwork — in exchange for a customer that is legally required to buy and legally required to pay.

Slower but bulletproof beats easy money that never shows up.


═══ THE NUMBER ═══

Here's the number that should stop you.

Last year, the federal government awarded over a hundred eighty-three billion dollars in contracts specifically to small businesses.

Not giant corporations. Small businesses. Companies like yours.

And that's not a "nice to have" for them. It's the law. The government is required to put a share of its spending into small businesses every year — so this money isn't a favor you have to beg for. It's a target somebody in that building has to hit.

To put a hundred eighty-three billion in perspective —

the Department of Defense alone spent four hundred forty-six billion dollars on contracts.

The Department of Veterans Affairs — sixty-seven billion.

Energy — forty-six billion.

Health and Human Services — thirty billion.

This is the largest, most reliable buyer in the history of the world. And a chunk of it is set aside, by law, for the small business owner watching this right now.


═══ THE MYTH ═══

Now let's kill the myth.

You think they're only buying fighter jets and rockets. They're not.

Last year the government spent thirty-eight billion dollars just on facilities support — basically running and maintaining buildings.

Almost six billion on security guards.

Two billion on janitorial services. Cleaning.

Over a billion on food service. Feeding people.

Landscaping. Plumbing. Heating and air conditioning.

These are not high-tech businesses. These are boring, ordinary, everyday businesses.

The same kind of business somebody in your town is running right now — except that person is billing the one customer that can't ghost them and can't stiff them, and their competitor down the street has no idea it's even possible.


═══ WHY NOBODY TALKS ABOUT IT ═══

So why doesn't anybody talk about this?

Three reasons.

Number one — it sounds boring. "Government contracting" makes people's eyes glaze over. Nobody's making it exciting, so it stays invisible.

Number two — the people who ARE doing it have zero incentive to tell you. Why would they? Less competition for them.

And number three — everyone assumes it's harder than it is. They picture a wall of forms. But the front door is simpler than you think, and I'll point you to it in a second.

That's the whole game. It's hidden in plain sight. A hundred eighty-three billion dollars, sitting right there — because the branding is bad and nobody bothered to look.


═══ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU ═══

So what does this actually mean for you?

It means the customer you've been ignoring might be the biggest one you'll ever have.

It means you don't need a new business. You need a new buyer for the business you already have.

The cleaning company. The IT service. The staffing agency. The construction crew. The consultant.

Whatever you do — there's a real chance the government is already buying it from somebody. The only question is whether it's you.

Think about what that does to your business. One customer that's legally required to buy, legally required to pay, and publishes exactly what it needs before it needs it. That's not a lead you chase. That's a buyer who already put its hand up.


═══ THE TURN — HOW YOU'D FIND IT ═══

And that's the part people get stuck on.

"Okay Eric, that sounds great — but how do I actually see what they're buying? Where do I even look?"

Fair question.

All of this spending is public. Every contract, every dollar, every agency — it's all out in the open, by law. Remember: this customer can't ghost you. It literally publishes what it's buying. The problem is just knowing where to look and what to do first.

So I put the whole starting playbook into a free guide. How to get in the system, how to find what they're buying in your industry, and the exact first moves — no fluff, no cost.

The link's in the description. Grab it, and you'll have the real roadmap in front of you tonight.


═══ CLOSE ═══

Look — I've watched ordinary people build serious businesses on this one idea.

Not because they were smarter. Not because they had connections. Just because they looked where nobody else was looking.

Here's the whole thing in one line: every other creator teaches you how to MAKE money — nobody hands you the customer. I'm handing you the customer. The one that's legally required to buy, can't ghost you, can't stiff you. A hundred eighty-three billion dollars a year, and most people will never even glance at it.

Don't be most people.

Grab the free first-contract guide in the description — it's at getmindy.ai — and take the first step tonight.

And subscribe, because I'm doing a whole series on winning this the real way, step by step.

I'll see you in the next one.
