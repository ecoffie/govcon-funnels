★ The live middle — narrate, don't read

Run sheet

7 beats · react honestly · [SCREEN] cues stay visible · dry-run first
▶
Before you go live: log into Mindy at getmindy.ai, confirm it loads, and do a full private dry-run of every search below so you know exactly what comes back. Zero fumbling on camera — but keep the take itself unedited, one continuous middle. Public search views only — never show a private/account-only screen.
1

Pick the business (out loud, on screen)

Say: "I'm going to pick a real, boring business. A two-person commercial cleaning company. No trucks, no warehouse, no fancy equipment — a mop, a vacuum, and two people. If the government buys from a business THIS ordinary, it'll buy from yours."

[SCREEN: Mindy open, blank search. Cursor in the box. Let them see you haven't pre-loaded anything.]

2

Type it in, live

Say: "Watch. I'm typing it in right now — 'commercial cleaning' — and I'm going to hit enter and we find out together."

[SCREEN: type "commercial cleaning" / "janitorial" → hit enter → let it load on camera. Don't talk over the load. Let the number land.]

3

The reveal (this pays off the title)

Say: "Look at that. It just handed us the whole thing. That's how much the government spends on cleaning — for a two-person business. And look who's buying — these agencies right here, by name." Then: "I didn't search for this. I didn't dig. I typed two words and it handed me the customers — the exact agencies already paying people to do this."

[SCREEN: point the cursor at (a) the total spend number, (b) a few named agencies/buyers, (c) any contract or opportunity rows visible. React honestly to the real figure — expect it large (billions across the category). Only say "here are the contracts" if live solicitation rows are actually on screen — otherwise anchor on the spend total + named buyers, which the tool reliably shows.]

4

Widen out (prove it wasn't a fluke)

Say: "Okay, maybe cleaning's a fluke. Let's try a couple more, live." On the random one: "Let's try something you'd SWEAR they don't buy." If it comes back low, SAY SO: "See — not everything's huge, and I'll show you the honest ones too. But look how much still is."

[SCREEN: quickly type 2–3 more relatable ones — "IT services" / "software," "catering" / "food service," and ONE risky/random one (landscaping, pest control, photography).]

5

By location — "this could be near YOU"

Say: "Here's the part that gets people. Let's not search WHAT — let's search WHERE." Then: "This is money moving through a place like yours. Not DC. Not some mega-city. Your county."

[SCREEN: search a mid-size city or county. Point at the local dollars.]

6

"Here's what you'd actually DO with this"

Say: "So what do you do with a screen like this? You don't admire it. You pick ONE agency that's already buying what you do, you find who they buy it from, and you go be the next person on that list. That's it. The tool just did the hardest part — it found who's spending — for free."

[SCREEN: point once more at a named buyer / a contract row to make "who to go after" concrete.]

7

Pause and play (the participation hook)

Say: "Now pause this video and type in YOUR business. Right now. I'll wait."

[SCREEN: hold a beat on the search box. Full-screen lower-third: "PAUSE — type in yours." Then → switch back to camera for the CLOSE.]

⚠
Keep it unedited. Do not cut the middle into a slick montage — the raw, one-take "whatever comes back, comes back" is the entire trust mechanic. If a search is underwhelming, leave it in and react honestly. That's the point.
