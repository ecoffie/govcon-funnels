Run sheet

Type these in order · react honestly · dry-run first
▶
Before you go live: log into Mindy, confirm it loads, and do a full private dry-run of every search below so you know what comes back. On camera you want zero fumbling — but keep the take itself unedited. Public search views only — never show a private/account-only screen.
1

Cleaning / janitorial

Say: "Let's start with the most boring thing possible — cleaning."

DO: type "janitorial" or "cleaning" → point at the total + a few buyers. Expect billions. React: "the government spends BILLIONS on cleaning."

2

Staffing

Say: "What if you don't do the work yourself — you just supply people?"

DO: type "staffing" / "temporary help" → show the buyers. Tie to "you supply the workers."

3

IT / tech

Say: "Okay, something less boring — IT."

DO: type "IT services" / "software" → show scale. Contrast with #1 ("boring AND tech, both huge").

4

Catering / food

Say: "Something anybody could start — food."

DO: type "catering" / "food service" → point at buyers + $. "A catering business like the one in your town."

5

A viewer-suggested / risky one

Say: "Let's try something random — something you'd swear the government doesn't buy."

DO: type something unexpected (landscaping, pest control, photography). If it's low, SAY SO — the honesty is the trust. "See — not everything's huge. But look how much still is."

6

By location — "your county"

Say: "Now the part that gets people — let's look by PLACE."

DO: search a location (a mid-size city / county). Show the local $. "This could be near YOU." Bridges to the participatory ask.

7

The pause-and-play beat

Say: "Pause this video right now and type in YOUR business. I'll wait."

DO: hold a beat. This is the participation hook — the whole reason the format converts. Then → switch back to camera for the CLOSE.

⚠
Keep it unedited. Do not cut the middle into a slick montage — the raw, one-take "whatever comes back, comes back" is the entire trust mechanic. If a search is underwhelming, leave it in and react honestly. That's the point.
