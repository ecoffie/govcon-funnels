═══ COLD OPEN — CHALLENGE THE BELIEF ═══

Everybody treats the big customer like a gatekeeper — someone to get past. A wall between you and the money, whose whole job is to keep you out. So you spend all your energy trying to get past them.

But I sat down with the people who actually control the budget, and they told me the opposite: they're desperate to spend it, and they can't find enough good people to spend it on.

And the biggest buyer on earth — the U.S. government — spends three-quarters of a trillion dollars a year looking for people like you. So I went and interviewed the ones on the OTHER side of that desk. Former contracting officers who personally signed off on billions. The small-business directors at the Navy and the National Guard.

And I asked them straight up: are you trying to keep us out?

Every single one of them told me the opposite. They're not guarding the door. They're holding it open — and they can't find enough of us to walk through it.

Four of them. Never met each other. Listen.

[CLIP: Lori Smith — "There's this myth that I absolutely deplore — this assumption that all of government is trying to purposely keep people out. But there is no policy written out, in black and white, that says do this, and don't do this." @locate-on-tape · Ep 165]

A myth she deplores. There is no rule that says keep you out. The wall you've been picturing — it isn't in the regulations.


═══ CLIP 3 — KYLE BEAGLE ═══

Okay — but do they actually need you? Kyle Beagle runs the small business programs for the entire National Guard Bureau. Offices in every state and territory. Here's the problem he's actually dealing with.

Whole regions where they can't get enough bids on construction. Bases that can't find someone to cater a weekend drill. He is not turning vendors away. He is asking where the vendors ARE.


═══ CLIP 4 — LESLIE FAIRCLOTH ═══

And then there's Leslie Faircloth — the small-business director for the Navy's training systems division. They call her the Small Business Jedi Master. When she stands in front of a room full of contractors, this is what she asks them to do.

[CLIP: Leslie Faircloth — "At every event I ask them: help me help you. If you see something on this list that you're interested in — either as a prime or a sub — let me know." @21:32 · Ep 250]

Help me help you. And in case you still believe she's already picked the winner before you ever walk in the room:

[CLIP: Leslie Faircloth — "99.9% of the time we really don't know who we're trying to get to… we want competition." @56:39 · Ep 250]

Ninety-nine point nine percent of the time, they don't know who they want. They're hoping it's you.
