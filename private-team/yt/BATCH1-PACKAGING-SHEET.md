# Batch 1 — Locked Packaging Sheet

Every episode run through the [Packaging Framework](./PACKAGING-FRAMEWORK.md) Pyramid before it ships:
**belief shift · one dominant emotion · ≤4-word thumbnail · story-title · one-sentence hook.**

**The spine (thread one line into every cold-open or CTA):** *Every other creator teaches you how to MAKE money — nobody hands you the customer. We hand you the customer: one that's legally required to buy, can't ghost you, can't stiff you (just slower + more paperwork). Slower but bulletproof beats easy money.*

**The batch-level gap to fix:** Batch 1 is almost entirely *solo/topic* video — strong on Secrets/Money/Lists, but it contains **no transformation/empire story** (a person's before→after). That's the exact franchise the framework names as the channel's edge and the real 200K bet. **Recommendation:** make the open Ep 01 slot — or the first Batch 2 slot — a full documentary-cut interview, so the scoreboard actually tests the thesis instead of only the curiosity lanes.

## Ep 02 · How I'd Win My First $100K Contract · Converter (warm → Mindy)

- **Belief shift:** Everyone thinks you need experience, connections, and capital to win a government contract → Actually you need a free ID and one small bid — and the step most how-tos skip: *submitting.*
- **Emotion:** Opportunity ("I could actually do this")
- **Title (upgrade the "How I'd…" topic framing):**
  - ✅ **If I Started Over, This Is My First $100K**
  - How I'd Win $100K With Zero Experience
  - The Step Everyone Skips When Chasing Their First Contract
- **Thumbnail (≤4 words):** `$0 → $100K` · or `FIRST $100K`
- **Hook:** "If I had to start over — no experience, no connections, no money — here's exactly how I'd win a hundred-thousand-dollar contract this year, *because the one customer that's legally required to buy doesn't care about your résumé.*"
- **Note:** Solo → no transformation; carry it on belief-shift + opportunity. Warm lane, so CTA is direct Mindy sign-up.

## Ep 04 · Veterans: The Government Will Literally Pay You · Floor (proven — prior cut hit 16K)

- **Belief shift:** Everyone thinks veterans have to *pay* for training to start a business → Actually the government will literally **pay you**, and set aside ~$33B in contracts for you.
- **Emotion:** Curiosity ("VA Pays You" — the archetype)
- **Title:** ✅ **Veterans: The Government Will Literally Pay You** *(keep — already framework-perfect)*
- **Thumbnail:** `VA PAYS YOU` ✅
- **Hook:** "If you served, the government built an entire system to *pay you* to start a business — money, training, mentorship, and a customer that specifically wants to buy from you."
- **Landmines:** VetCert moved VA → **SBA** (Jan 2023); the goal is now **5%**, not 3%. High share — veterans tag each other.
