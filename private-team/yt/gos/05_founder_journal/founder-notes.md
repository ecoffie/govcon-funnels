## Founder Note #001 · The Product
Media is not our product. Government contracts are not our product. Courses are not our product. **Our product is entrepreneurs.** Every story exists to move someone closer to becoming a government entrepreneur.

## Founder Note #002 · We Don't Create Opportunity
We reveal it. Opportunity already exists — stories make it visible.

## Founder Note #003 · Every Video Should Make the Company Smarter
The audience receives value. The company receives knowledge. Every upload must improve both.

## Founder Note #004 · We Build Systems, Not Videos
Videos disappear into the algorithm. Systems compound forever.

## Founder Note #005 · The Y-Combinator Insight
People think Y Combinator funds startups — it doesn't; it runs a *system* that repeatedly creates founders. The startups are the output; the system is the product. GovCon Giants is the same for **government entrepreneurs.**

## Founder Note #006 · The One Obsession Is Belief
Toyota's obsession is quality; Amazon's is the customer; MrBeast's is attention. Ours is **belief.** Every edit is judged by one question — *did it increase belief?*
