# Movie Poster Test™  ·  v0.2
### GOS 01_Method · the final quality gate before packaging

> *If Netflix bought this as a documentary and hung one poster in Times Square, what would it say?* Not the plot — **the promise.** If the poster doesn't make someone stop walking, the thumbnail won't make them stop scrolling.

## The test
**Our Movie Poster (≤ 4 words):** ________________________

- [ ] It's **4 words or fewer**
- [ ] It's the **promise**, not the topic or the plot
- [ ] It uses **one** formula: `OLD → NEW` · `NO → YES` · `STOP` · `MONEY` · `QUESTION`
- [ ] It carries **one** emotion
- [ ] It could open with *"Everyone thinks…"* / *"Nobody told me…"* — never *"Interview with…"* / *"Episode N…"*
- [ ] **Belief check:** does it *increase belief*? (the one obsession)

**If any box is unchecked, the story isn't packaged yet — go back to the [Story DNA Worksheet](./story-dna-worksheet.md).**

## Proven posters
`VA PAYS YOU` · `BANK SAID NO` · `BUY, DON'T BUILD` · `YOU'RE DOING 8(a) WRONG` · `ONE PRISON CHANGED EVERYTHING`

*The poster drives the thumbnail and the title (Packaging Matrix™). Weak poster → weak thumbnail → do not publish.*
