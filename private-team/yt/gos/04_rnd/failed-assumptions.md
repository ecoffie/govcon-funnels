# 04 · R&D — Failed Assumptions

*Straight from SpaceX: every failed assumption is documented, not hidden — **celebrated.** A disproven belief is a permanent upgrade to the system. 300 of these and the company is very hard to beat.*

**Format:** the assumption → the result → the system change.

---

### Failed Assumption #001 · "Shorter titles win."
- **Result:** FALSE. Across 1,759 videos, winners average **10.2 words**, buried videos **10.6** — statistically the same. Length is not the lever.
- **System change:** Packaging Matrix rule — don't *shorten* the title, **re-aim** it (topic → promise, at the same length).

### Failed Assumption #002 · "Naming the guest in the title helps."
- **Result:** FALSE. Only **2%** of winners name the guest vs **6%** of buried videos. The guest's name is not a hook; their story is.
- **System change:** Title rule — cut the guest name from the title.

### Failed Assumption #017 · "Educational titles outperform story titles."
- **Result:** FALSE. Story titles generated significantly more interest.
- **System change:** Update the Packaging Matrix — package the story, not the lesson.

---

*Seeded July 18, 2026. #001 and #002 are validated from our own catalog; #017 is carried from the framework. Every future experiment that disproves a belief lands here.*
