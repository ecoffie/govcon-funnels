# 04 · R&D — Decision Journal™

*Every major decision documented — not for history, for **learning.** Each: the question, the decision, the reason, the expected result, a review date, and (later) the outcome. Bezos-style: write the decision down, then check whether the data agreed. 500 of these over five years is a record of how the company learned to think.*

---

### Decision #001 · Adopt GOS as the single source of truth
- **Question:** Do we keep ideas in chat/docs, or build one versioned operating system?
- **Decision:** Build **GOS** (GovCon Operating System). Every idea has a home; new ideas are incorporated under the right module, never in a new document.
- **Reason:** Unorganized IP becomes an un-navigable transcript. Coherence must be engineered now, while it's small.
- **Expected result:** Faster onboarding; nothing lost; the system compounds.
- **Review:** Ongoing. **Outcome:** —

### Decision #002 · Package around transformation, not process
- **Question:** Do we package videos around the *process/education* or the *transformation*?
- **Decision:** **Transformation.**
- **Reason:** Every winning video has centered on transformation, not education.
- **Expected result:** Higher CTR.
- **Review:** 30 days. **Outcome:** Early support — the VA repackage (topic → transformation) drove ~125× view velocity on the same footage.

### Decision #003 · Rename "Frameworks" → "Systems"
- **Decision:** The frameworks are **Systems** (Story DNA System™, Hook Engine System™, Movie Poster System™, Editing System™, Learning System™).
- **Reason:** A framework reads as *a tool*; a System reads as *how the company operates.* Systems produce results and create identity.
- **Review:** Rolls through the docs over v0.2. **Outcome:** —
