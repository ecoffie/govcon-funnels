### Discovery #001 · Government Entrepreneurs Are the Product
- **Status:** Validated
- **Evidence:** Anthony · Jackson · Shane · the VA video — every breakout is a *transformation*, not a lesson.
- **Implications:** Content · Education · Community · Events · Hiring all reorient around one output.
- **System changes:** Updated Mission, Frameworks, and Production to serve entrepreneur-creation.

### Discovery #002 · Transformation Is the Product; Government Contracting Is the Vehicle
- **Status:** Validated
- **Evidence:** The VA video — same footage, repackaged from a topic to a transformation → ~125× monthly view velocity.
- **System changes:** Packaging Matrix rule — *package the transformation, never the topic.*

### Discovery #003 · Every Video Must Make the Company Smarter
- **Status:** Adopted
- **Implication:** The audience receives value; the company receives knowledge. Every upload feeds the Learning System.

### Discovery #004 · The Speed of Learning Determines the Speed of Growth
- **Status:** Operating principle
- **Implication:** We optimize learning, not output. A company that learns faster than its competitors becomes impossible to beat.

### Discovery #005 · Packaging Is the Only Variable
- **Status:** Validated (data)
- **Evidence:** Across 1,759 videos, the buried interviews out-engage our biggest breakout (10–16% vs 5.1%) — the content is *better*; only packaging starves the reach. **0% of winners open with an episode number; 28% of buried videos do.**
- **System changes:** The Production System's evidence-backed packaging rules; the Repurposing Queue (994 buried videos worth re-packaging).
