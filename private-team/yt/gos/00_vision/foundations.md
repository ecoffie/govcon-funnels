## Core Belief
> **We are not building a YouTube channel. We are building a company that creates Government Entrepreneurs.**

GovCon Giants is not a media company. Media is the delivery mechanism · Education is the vehicle · Community is the multiplier. **The product is Government Entrepreneurs.**

## Our Mission
> **Build Government Entrepreneurs.** Everything we create should help someone move one step closer to becoming one.

## Our Purpose
We reveal opportunities that already exist. We tell stories that create belief. We build systems that create entrepreneurs.

## First Principle
> **Opportunity is only valuable when it is discovered.** GovCon Giants exists to make invisible opportunities visible.

## Central Thesis (The GovCon Giants Method™)
> **Opportunity changes lives. Stories unlock opportunity.**


## The Laws (v0.1)
1. **Opportunity** — People cannot pursue opportunities they cannot see.
2. **Belief** — People act only after they believe change is possible.
3. **Evidence** — People trust proof more than promises.
4. **Transformation** — Transformation is the product; government contracting is the vehicle.
5. **Identity** — People pursue who they want to become.
6. **Simplicity** — Complex opportunities spread through simple stories.
7. **Curiosity** — Curiosity opens the door; education keeps people inside.
8. **Conflict** — Conflict creates attention; attention creates learning.
9. **Legacy** — Every success story should create another success story.
10. **Multiplication** — Every entrepreneur becomes evidence that creates another entrepreneur.

*Candidate laws under review (see [Decision Journal #003](../04_rnd/decision-journal.md)): Proximity · Contradiction · First Step · Trust.*
