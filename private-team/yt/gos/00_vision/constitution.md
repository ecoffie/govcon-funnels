# The GovCon Giants Constitution
### v0.1 · GOS 00_Vision · July 18, 2026

*Every great company has one. Amazon has Leadership Principles. Netflix has Culture. Bridgewater has Principles. Toyota has The Toyota Way. This is ours. It is short and timeless. Everything else — production, editing, packaging, analytics — flows from it. Every future decision references it.*

---

## Article I · Why We Exist

We exist to **build government entrepreneurs.**

Not to make videos, teach courses, or win contracts. Those are outputs. The product is the entrepreneur. Media is the delivery mechanism, education is the vehicle, community is the multiplier — but the thing we manufacture is a person who acts on an opportunity they could not see before.

**First Principle:** *Opportunity is only valuable when it is discovered.* Opportunity already exists — the federal marketplace, the veteran programs, the set-asides have existed for generations. Our work is to make the invisible visible. GovCon Giants exists to reveal.

## Article II · What We Believe

> **Opportunity changes lives. Stories unlock opportunity.**

We believe people cannot pursue what they cannot see (**Opportunity**); that they act only after they believe change is possible (**Belief**); that they trust proof over promises (**Evidence**); that they are moved by transformation, not information (**Transformation**); that they pursue who they want to become (**Identity**); that complexity spreads through simple stories (**Simplicity**); that curiosity opens the door (**Curiosity**); that without conflict there is no transformation (**Conflict**); that every success story should create another (**Legacy**); and that every entrepreneur becomes evidence that creates the next (**Multiplication**).

These are the **Laws** — truths about people, not opinions about media. They must still be true 100 years from now.

## Article III · How We Make Decisions

Before we do anything, we answer five questions:
1. Does it **reveal an opportunity**?
2. Does it **build belief**?
3. Does it **create a government entrepreneur**?
4. Does it **improve the operating system**?
5. Will we **learn something even if it fails**?

**Yes to at least four of five → we move.** Otherwise, we rethink.

Two standing rules of decision-making:
- **Belief is the single obsession.** Toyota's is quality; Amazon's is the customer; ours is belief. Every edit, title, and thumbnail is judged by one question — *did it increase belief?* Not *was it entertaining* or *educational.*
- **Producers decide the story; editors amplify it.** The story is found first, then edited. That is how documentaries are made.

## Article IV · What We Never Compromise

- **We choose hope over hype.** Reality is compelling enough; we never exaggerate an opportunity.
- **We build trust before traffic.** A million views without trust is worth little. In a field built on credibility, reputation is the moat — we never trade it for a click.
- **We teach through evidence, not opinion.** We show people who have done it.
- **We respect complexity.** We simplify the opportunity; we never simplify the truth.
- **We create entrepreneurs, not consumers.** The highest compliment is never "great video." It is *"I started my business because of your channel."*
- **The guest is the guide; the viewer is the hero.** We never make the story about our own importance.

## Article V · How We Define Success

We measure **learning, not output** — and above all, **Entrepreneurs Created.** Not views, subscribers, or downloads. We track *journeys*, not videos: a roster of Builders moving one stage at a time from Unaware → Aware → Belief → Action → First Win → Identity → Entrepreneur → Leader → Mentor.

Every piece of content has one job: **move one person one stage.** Not zero to millionaire — one step.

## Article VI · How We Improve

We build the company like a software company: **everything is versioned.** Nobody expects perfection; they expect evolution. We ship v0.1, run it on a real video this week, and release a better version every Friday.

- **Every story should improve the operating system.** Every employee's real job description contains one line: *improve GOS.* Editing, booking, designing are *how*; leaving the system better than you found it is the *job.*
- **We document how we decide** (Decision Journal), **what we learn** (Learning Journal), **what we discover** (99_Discoveries), and **what we were wrong about** (Failed Assumptions — celebrated, never hidden, straight from SpaceX).
- **Learn faster than everyone else.** *A company that learns faster than its competitors eventually becomes impossible to compete with.* Speed of learning determines speed of growth.
- **Build assets that compound.** The Method, the Systems, the Databases, the Libraries — each turns a one-off into a permanent capability.
