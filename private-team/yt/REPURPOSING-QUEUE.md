## What the catalog audit found (1,759 videos, 54.7K subs, 3.9M lifetime views)

- **994 of 1,759 videos have under 1,000 views.** 231 have under 500. That's the underutilized tail.
- **356 interviews (20 min+, 1yr+ old) sit under 6,000 views** — the repurposing pool.
- **The engagement paradox that proves the thesis:** the buried interviews run **10–16% like+comment engagement**. The VA breakout winner runs **5.1%**. The audience response on the buried videos is *2–3× stronger per view* than the video that just did 34K — they're better content, starved of reach purely by packaging. Packaging is the only variable.

## Workflow (per repurposed video)
1. Pick a starved interview with a real transformation or hidden advantage on tape.
2. Run it through the [Pyramid](./PACKAGING-FRAMEWORK.md#pillar-9): belief shift → hidden secret → conflict → lesson → one-sentence hook.
3. New title (starts with "Everyone thinks…" / "Nobody told me…"), ≤4-word thumbnail, transformation description.
4. Optional: trim the long cut (the VA repackage went 55:36 → 43:59).
5. Log the before→after views so we can prove the lift, same as the VA case study.
