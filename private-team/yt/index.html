What the scoreboard decides — the Batch 2 rule

Reallocate toward the ceiling you actually hit

A WIDE swing breaks 200K→ make 5+ siblings of that exact hook. This is the 1M path. (Ep 03 / 05 / 07 / 08 lane.)
NEWS wins reach→ build a standing news/policy cadence — fastest to film, evergreen-refreshable. (Ep 01 / 06 lane.)
A CONVERTER wins signups→ that's the money bucket — make more live-tool + case-study videos even if view-peak is lower. (Ep 02 / 10 lane.)
Nothing breaks 100K→ go wider, not safer — the escalation group.
Read both signalsPeak views = growth lane. /youtube signups = money lane. They may differ — fund both, chase the breakout hardest.
The loop: Publish Batch 1 → watch ~3–4 weeks → the winning row picks the lane → build 5+ siblings of the winner for Batch 2 → cut the flops. The winning lane's share should grow to 5–6 of the next 10. That's how the channel compounds toward 1M.
