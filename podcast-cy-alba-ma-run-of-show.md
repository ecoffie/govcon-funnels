## Guest Bio (Reference)

Isaias "Cy" Alba, IV is a partner at PilieroMazza PLLC, advising small and mid-sized government contractors on SBA compliance, teaming and joint ventures, mentor-protégé agreements, bid protests (GAO, COFC, Federal Circuit), SBA size and status protests, OHA appeals, Contract Disputes Act claims, REAs, terminations for convenience, suspensions and debarments, OCI mitigation, FAR/DFARS data rights and IP, ITAR compliance, and govcon-focused M&A (transactions up to ~$100M).

**Experience:** Nearly 20 years at PilieroMazza (Partner since Jan 2012). Prior: Saltman & Stevens (now Smith Currie & Hancock). Internships at the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission and U.S. Department of State.

**Education:** J.D., cum laude, Catholic University — Columbus School of Law. B.A. Philosophy, Saint Joseph's University.

**Recognition:** Super Lawyers — Government Contracts Law (Rising Star 2013–2019; Super Lawyers 2023, 2025).

## 6:00 – 18:00 | PART 1 — Prepare to Sell (or Buy)

**Goal:** Get transaction-ready before LOI.

**Anchor question:**
> "Cy, if a founder tells you they want to sell in 18 months, what's the first thing you tell them to fix?"

**Follow-ups:**

- What makes govcon M&A fundamentally different from a regular commercial deal?
- What are buyers actually paying for in govcon — is it contracts, compliance, people, customer access, or something else?
- What does "clean" look like? Contract files, compliance records, SBA status, OCI documentation — what should be in order?
- What are the biggest pre-LOI mistakes you see that destroy valuation?
- If a founder wants to maximize value 6–12 months out, what moves actually move the needle?

**Mini-summary before transition:**
> "So step one — clean house before you ever talk to a buyer. Now let's talk about what happens when the deal gets real."

---

## 18:00 – 33:00 | PART 2 — Diligence + Deal Structuring

**Goal:** Show how deals are won or lost in diligence and legal structure.

**Anchor question:**
> "When diligence starts, what are the top 3 red flags that make you tell a client to pause or reprice the deal?"

**Follow-ups:**

- Asset deal vs equity deal in govcon — how should founders think about that choice?
- Novation and change-of-control — what do most people underestimate about timing and agency consent?
- SBA eligibility and affiliation — how easy is it to accidentally break small business status during a deal?
- On FAR/DFARS — what data rights, IP, or subcontractor dependencies cause the biggest surprises?
- In the purchase agreement itself, what reps, warranties, indemnities, escrows, or earn-outs matter most in govcon deals?
- How should earn-outs be structured when so much future value depends on recompetes and option years?

**Mini-summary before transition:**
> "So diligence isn't just financial — in govcon, the legal and compliance layer is where the real value sits. Last piece — what happens after you close."
