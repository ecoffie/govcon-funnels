What is an IDIQ?

An IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity) contract is a pre-competed contract vehicle that allows agencies to issue task orders for specific work. Think of it as a “hunting license” — once you’re on the vehicle, you can compete for task orders with a much smaller pool of competitors (typically 5–10 companies instead of 50–100).

IDIQs have a ceiling value (the maximum that can be spent) and an ordering period (how long agencies can issue task orders). When the ordering period ends, the agency rebids — that’s your opportunity to get on the next one.

Two ways to participate: (1) Get ON the vehicle yourself by winning a spot during the competition, or (2) Team with a prime who’s already on the vehicle as their subcontractor. Most small businesses start with option 2.

1
GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS)
Formerly GSA Schedule, GSA IT Schedule 70, Professional Services Schedule
What It Is
The largest and most well-known government-wide contract vehicle. A pre-negotiated pricing agreement that allows any federal agency to buy your products or services at GSA-approved rates through GSA Advantage or eBuy.
Who It’s For
Any small business that sells products or services the government buys. Categories (SINs) cover IT, professional services, facilities, security, HR, financial, scientific, and more.
Ceiling Value
No overall ceiling — each contract has its own ceiling based on sales history.
How to Get On
Submit an offer through GSA’s eOffer system. Timeline: 3–6 months. GSA charges 0.75% Industrial Funding Fee.
Pro Tip

Start here. GSA MAS is the most accessible vehicle for small businesses. Once you have a schedule, agencies can buy from you directly. Task orders flow through GSA eBuy.

2
OASIS+
One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus • Managed by GSA
What It Is
A government-wide, multi-agency IDIQ for complex professional services across all domains — management consulting, scientific, engineering, financial, health, IT, environmental, and more.
Who It’s For
Service-based businesses. Has specific pools: Small Business, 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB, and Unrestricted.
Ceiling Value
$100 billion+ across all pools.
How to Get On
GSA opens periodic on-ramp windows. Check sam.gov and gsa.gov for announcements.
Pro Tip

OASIS+ is one of the highest-value vehicles for professional services firms. If you qualify, get on it. If you don’t qualify yet, team with a prime who’s already on it.

3
CIO-SP4
Chief Information Officer – Solutions and Partners 4 • Managed by NIH NITAAC
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ for IT solutions: cloud computing, cybersecurity, software development, health IT, IT operations, and emerging technologies.
Who It’s For
IT companies. Has pools for: Small Business, 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB. Also has a mentor-protégé joint venture pool.
Ceiling Value
$50 billion over 10 years.
How to Get On
Recently awarded. On-ramps may open periodically. Monitor nitaac.nih.gov.
Pro Tip

If you’re in IT, this is one of the most important vehicles. Many primes on CIO-SP4 actively seek small business teaming partners.

4
Alliant 3 / Alliant 3 Small Business
Managed by GSA
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ for complex IT services: infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud, application development, systems integration, and managed services.
Who It’s For
IT companies with significant past performance. Alliant 3 Small Business has lower thresholds but still requires demonstrated IT experience.
Ceiling Value
$75 billion (estimated) over 10 years.
How to Get On
Solicitation released. Check gsa.gov/alliant for current status and future on-ramps.
Pro Tip

Alliant is a high-bar vehicle. If you’re not ready yet, identify Alliant holders in your NAICS and approach them for teaming.

5
SEWP VI
Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement VI • Managed by NASA • Pronounced “soup”
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ for IT products (hardware, software, cloud services) and product-based services. Known for fast ordering — agencies love it because procurement is streamlined.
Who It’s For
IT product resellers, OEMs, cloud service providers. Group A (small business) and Group D (all businesses).
Ceiling Value
$40 billion+ across all groups.
How to Get On
On-ramps open periodically. Check sewp.nasa.gov. Requires IT product sales experience and manufacturer authorizations.
Pro Tip

If you sell IT products or cloud services, SEWP is essential. It’s one of the fastest ordering vehicles in government.

6
8(a) STARS III
Managed by GSA
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ exclusively for 8(a) certified small businesses, focused on IT services: cloud, cybersecurity, software development, data analytics, and emerging technologies.
Who It’s For
8(a) certified small businesses ONLY.
Ceiling Value
$50 billion over 10 years.
How to Get On
Check gsa.gov for on-ramp announcements. Must have active 8(a) certification.
Pro Tip

If you’re 8(a), get on this vehicle as soon as on-ramps open. The competition pool is limited to 8(a) firms, so win rates are higher. One of the best advantages of 8(a) certification.

7
VETS 2
Managed by GSA
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ exclusively for Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (SDVOSB), covering IT services: cloud, cybersecurity, systems integration, IT consulting, and managed services.
Who It’s For
SDVOSB-certified businesses ONLY.
Ceiling Value
$5 billion over 5 years (with 5-year option).
How to Get On
Check gsa.gov for on-ramp announcements. Must be verified as SDVOSB through SBA.
Pro Tip

Similar to 8(a) STARS III but for veterans. Limited competition pool means higher win rates. If you’re SDVOSB and in IT, this should be a priority.

8
HCaTS
Human Capital and Training Solutions • Managed by GSA
What It Is
A government-wide IDIQ for human capital services: training, organizational development, HR consulting, workforce planning, learning management, and leadership development.
Who It’s For
Companies providing training, HR, and organizational development services. Has Small Business and Unrestricted pools.
Ceiling Value
$11.5 billion over 10 years.
How to Get On
Awarded. Monitor gsa.gov for on-ramp opportunities. Requires human capital/training past performance.
Pro Tip

If you’re in training, HR consulting, or organizational development, HCaTS is the primary vehicle. Primes frequently need small business teaming partners for specific training content areas.

9
Polaris
Small Business GWAC • Managed by GSA
What It Is
GSA’s newest small business IT GWAC. Covers the full range of IT services and solutions. Designed as a flexible vehicle for emerging technologies and non-traditional IT companies.
Who It’s For
Small businesses in IT. Has pools for: Small Business (general), SDVOSB, WOSB, and HUBZone. Also includes a mentor-protégé pool.
Ceiling Value
$36 billion over 10 years.
How to Get On
Relatively new. Monitor gsa.gov/polaris for additional on-ramp opportunities.
Pro Tip

Polaris is specifically designed for small businesses and emerging IT companies. If you missed CIO-SP4 or Alliant, Polaris may be your best path onto a major IT vehicle.

10
Agency-Specific IDIQs
BPAs, MACs, and Single-Award IDIQs • Managed by individual agencies
What It Is
Beyond the government-wide vehicles above, every major agency has its own IDIQs tailored to their mission. Examples: Army’s RS3, DHS EAGLE II, VA’s T4NG. These are often smaller, more accessible, and have less competition than big GWACs.
Who It’s For
Varies by vehicle. Many agency-specific IDIQs are set aside for small businesses and have lower past performance thresholds.
Ceiling Value
Varies widely — from $50M to $10B+.
How to Get On
Monitor SAM.gov for solicitations in your NAICS. Check agency OSDBU websites for forecasts.
Pro Tip

Agency-specific IDIQs are often the BEST entry point for small businesses. Less competition, lower barriers, and you build direct relationships with the agency. Don’t overlook these while chasing big GWACs.

What to Do Next

1

Identify which vehicles match your NAICS codes and business certifications.

2

Check if any of these vehicles have open on-ramp opportunities (search SAM.gov and the managing agency’s website).

3

For vehicles you’re not on yet, find primes who hold those vehicles and approach them for teaming. Use the Federal Contractor Database to identify primes and SBLO contacts.

4

Track which IDIQs are expiring in your NAICS — the Recompete Tracker has 6,900+ expiring contracts. When an IDIQ expires, the agency rebids it. That’s your chance.

5

Start responding to Sources Sought notices for upcoming IDIQs. Less than 5% of contractors respond — your response can influence the final solicitation.
