Why Respond to Sources Sought Notices?

Less than 5% of small businesses respond to Sources Sought (also called RFIs or market research notices). Responding puts you in the top 5% automatically.

More importantly, your response can influence how the agency writes the final solicitation — shaping the requirement to favor your capabilities.

Where to find them: SAM.gov → Contract Opportunities → filter by Notice Type: “Sources Sought” or “Special Notice” → filter by your NAICS codes.

Tips for a Strong Response

1

Keep it to 3–5 pages. Sources Sought responses should be concise. The agency is surveying the market, not evaluating proposals.

2

Answer THEIR questions, not yours. If the notice lists specific questions, answer those exactly. Don’t skip any.

3

Reference the notice number and title on every page.

4

Attach your capability statement as a separate PDF.

5

Submit before the deadline. Late responses are usually not accepted.

6

Follow up. After submitting, email the contracting officer to confirm receipt and offer to discuss your capabilities further.
