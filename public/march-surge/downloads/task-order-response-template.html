Volume I Technical Approach
TIP: This is the most important volume. Demonstrate that you understand the agency’s problem and have a specific, detailed plan to solve it. Describe your PLAN, not your company.

1.1 Understanding of the Requirement

Demonstrate that you’ve read and understood the SOW/PWS. Paraphrase in your own words. Identify key challenges. Show insight beyond what’s written.

[Your Company Name] understands that [Agency Name] requires [specific services/products described in SOW]. The core challenge is [state the problem in your words]. Our approach addresses this by [1–2 sentence summary of your solution].

1.2 Technical Approach and Methodology

TIP: Best examples are SIMILAR to the current task in size, scope, and complexity. Federal preferred, but commercial counts. Focus on outcomes and results.

Pre-Submission Checklist

Compliance: Does your proposal address EVERY requirement in the SOW/PWS?

Evaluation: Have you written to the evaluation criteria in Section M?

Page Limits: Are you within page limits for each volume?

Formatting: Font, margins, spacing match Section L requirements?

Key Personnel: Are all required key personnel named with resumes attached?

Past Performance: Are references current? Have you alerted them they may be contacted?

Pricing: Does format match contract type? Are rates within vehicle ceilings?

Attachments: Resumes, letters of commitment, certifications, org chart all attached?

Deadline: Will you submit at least 2 hours before the deadline?

Review: Has at least one other person reviewed the full proposal for errors?
