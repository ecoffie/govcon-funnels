What is Capture Management?

Capture management is the structured process of identifying, qualifying, and winning government contracts before a proposal is ever written. It bridges the gap between market awareness and proposal submission — turning leads into wins through deliberate, repeatable actions.

Unlike reactive bidding, capture management is proactive. It starts months or even years before an RFP drops, giving your team time to shape requirements, build customer relationships, and position competitively.

Most GovCon Firms Don't Train for Capture

Capture management is one of the most critical skills in government contracting — yet most firms treat it as something you either already know or pick up on the job. The result is a handful of overworked BD professionals carrying the entire pipeline while the rest of the team watches from the sidelines.

⚠️

Knowledge Concentrated in 1–2 People

Only one or two people in most firms truly understand capture. When they leave, the pipeline goes with them.

🕑

PMs Miss Recompete Signals

Program managers focused on delivery miss the early warning signs that a recompete is coming — losing valuable positioning time.

❌

Ops Staff Can't Qualify Opportunities

Operations and technical staff encounter potential opportunities daily but lack the framework to evaluate and escalate them.

📈

Reactive Instead of Proactive

Without capture training, firms default to chasing RFPs after release — competing blind against teams that have been positioning for months.

The result: reactive bidding, low win rates, and a pipeline that depends on one person's Rolodex.

Capture Management Skills Your Team Will Master

Our program covers the full spectrum of capture management, adapted for non-BD professionals who need practical, GovCon-specific skills they can apply immediately.

🔍

Opportunity Identification

Spot follow-on, expansion, and new opportunities using contract data, forecasts, and customer conversations. Learn to see pipeline potential where others see routine work.

📊

Market Research

Use SAM.gov contract data, USAspending.gov, and SBA tools to research agencies, track spending patterns, and identify upcoming procurements.

🤝

Customer Engagement

Build and maintain relationships with contracting officers, program managers, and end users. Understand what they need before the requirement is written.

👥

Competitive Analysis

Assess competitor strengths, weaknesses, and likely strategies. Position your team's win themes against the competitive landscape.

💰

Price-to-Win Basics

Understand pricing strategies for government contracts. Learn how pricing interacts with technical approach and past performance in evaluation.

📝

Proposal Support

Contribute meaningfully to proposal volumes and compliance matrices. Translate operational experience into compelling proposal content.

📋

Capture Reviews

Participate in gate reviews and bid/no-bid decisions with confidence. Understand the criteria that separate a smart pursuit from a wasted effort.

📈

Pipeline Management

Track and prioritize opportunities systematically. Build a qualified pipeline that gives leadership visibility into the firm's growth trajectory.
