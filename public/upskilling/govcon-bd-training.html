The BD Gap in Government Contracting

Most GovCon firms depend on a handful of people to drive all new business — while delivery teams sit on untapped intelligence every day.

In a typical government contracting company, business development falls on one or two people. Meanwhile, program managers, technical leads, and operations staff interact with government customers daily — hearing about upcoming requirements, budget shifts, and pain points before they ever hit SAM.gov.

This intelligence gap costs firms real revenue. Opportunities go unnoticed because the people closest to the customer aren't trained to recognize or escalate them. Competitors with broader BD awareness consistently win recompetes and capture new work.

Hiring a dedicated BD professional costs $130,000+ per year. Training your existing team to support business development costs a fraction of that — and starts producing results in weeks, not months.
