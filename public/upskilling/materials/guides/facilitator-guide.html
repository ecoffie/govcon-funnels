Facilitator Guide: 30-Day Foundation Program

Program Overview

Attribute Value
Program GovCon BD Upskilling - 30-Day Foundation
Duration 4 weeks
Format Live instructor-led (cohort-based)
Session Length 2-2.5 hours per session
Sessions 8 sessions (2 per week)
Completion Model Certificate of Completion (activity-based)

Facilitator Qualifications

Required Background

  • 5+ years GovCon BD, capture, or contracts experience
  • Hands-on experience with SAM.gov Contract Data, USAspending
  • Understanding of federal procurement process
  • Experience training or mentoring others

Recommended

  • NCMA certification (CFCM, CPCM) or equivalent
  • Capture management experience
  • Proposal management experience
  • Small business program familiarity

Session-by-Session Guide

Week 1, Session 1: Module 1.1 - GovCon Market Overview

Learning Objectives: 1. Describe the federal contracting market structure and size 2. Identify major federal agencies as buyers 3. Explain the federal budget cycle 4. Recognize small business program goals 5. Navigate USAspending.gov

Preparation: - [ ] Test USAspending.gov access - [ ] Prepare agency spending examples - [ ] Have budget cycle visual ready - [ ] Prepare small business goal chart

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
Market size 10 min $700B+ annually; world’s largest buyer
Agency structure 10 min DoD largest; know components vs. agencies
Budget cycle 15 min FY Oct-Sep; Q4 surge is real
Small business 10 min 23% goal; certifications open doors

Applied Exercise: Market Research with USAspending - Navigate to USAspending.gov - Select target agency, drill into spending - Complete Market Overview Worksheet

Common Questions: - “Where does classified spending show up?” → It doesn’t; SAM.gov Contract Data excludes classified - “What if an agency doesn’t meet small business goals?” → Pressure, but no penalties - “How accurate is USAspending data?” → Official data; slight reporting lag

Deliverable Check: Market Overview Worksheet completed

Week 1, Session 2: Module 1.2 - Buyer Intelligence Basics

Learning Objectives: 1. Navigate SAM.gov Contract Data to research historical awards 2. Identify key buyers using spending data 3. Analyze spending patterns by NAICS/PSC 4. Research incumbent contractors 5. Document intelligence in structured format

Preparation: - [ ] Test SAM.gov Contract Data access (requires free account) - [ ] Prepare sample searches - [ ] Have NAICS/PSC reference ready - [ ] Prepare incumbent research example

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
SAM.gov Contract Data navigation 15 min Contract Award Search; requires SAM.gov account
NAICS vs. PSC 10 min NAICS = industry; PSC = what was bought
Incumbent research 10 min Know who you’re displacing
USAspending profiles 10 min Easier trends view; no account required

Applied Exercise: Agency Research Report - Research target agency in SAM.gov Contract Data - Identify top contractors, NAICS codes - Complete Agency Research Report template

Common Questions: - “Why does SAM.gov require an account?” → Security & data access control; free to create - “How far back should I look?” → 3-5 years typical; depends on recompete timing - “What about subcontract data?” → Limited in SAM.gov Contract Data; harder to find

Deliverable Check: Agency Research Report completed

Week 2, Session 3: Module 2.1 - Opportunity Discovery

Learning Objectives: 1. Navigate SAM.gov for contract opportunities 2. Distinguish opportunity types (RFP, RFQ, RFI, Sources Sought) 3. Apply qualification criteria 4. Set up saved searches 5. Document opportunities in structured log

Preparation: - [ ] Test SAM.gov access - [ ] Prepare example searches by NAICS - [ ] Have opportunity type comparison ready - [ ] Prepare qualification matrix visual

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
SAM.gov navigation 15 min Filters are key; practice combinations
Opportunity types 10 min RFI/Sources Sought = respond to shape requirements
Qualification 10 min Not every opportunity is worth pursuing
Saved searches 10 min Set it and forget it; daily notifications

Applied Exercise: Build Opportunity Log - Search SAM.gov with target NAICS codes - Identify 10 relevant opportunities - Qualify each using matrix - Set up saved search

Common Questions: - “What’s the difference between RFQ and RFP?” → RFQ = price focus; RFP = full proposal - “Should we respond to every RFI?” → Only relevant ones; shapes future RFPs - “How early is too early to find opportunities?” → Never too early; forecasts help

Deliverable Check: Opportunity Log (10 opportunities) + Saved Search created

Week 2, Session 4: Module 2.2 - Customer Engagement Fundamentals

Learning Objectives: 1. Identify key customer touchpoints (OSDBU, contracting, program) 2. Explain industry days and capability briefings 3. Prepare for customer interactions 4. Document engagement activities 5. Recognize appropriate engagement practices

Preparation: - [ ] Prepare OSDBU directory - [ ] Find current industry day examples on SAM.gov - [ ] Have elevator pitch formula ready - [ ] Prepare ethics/compliance scenarios

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
OSDBU role 10 min Your gateway to agencies; use them
Industry days 10 min Show up prepared with questions
Capability briefings 10 min Educate, don’t sell; be helpful
Engagement ethics 15 min FAR 3.104; know the boundaries

Applied Exercise: Customer Engagement Plan - Research target agency OSDBU - Find 3 engagement opportunities - Draft elevator pitch - Create 90-day engagement timeline

Role Play Option: Practice elevator pitch (30 seconds per person)

Common Questions: - “Can I ask about specific RFPs in a cap brief?” → No; general capabilities only - “What do I bring to an industry day?” → Business cards, capability statement, questions - “How do I follow up appropriately?” → Email thank you; don’t be pushy

Deliverable Check: Customer Engagement Plan completed

Week 3, Session 5: Module 3.1 - BD Process Fundamentals

Learning Objectives: 1. Describe BD lifecycle (BD → Capture → Proposal) 2. Distinguish between phases 3. Identify pipeline stages and gate criteria 4. Explain teaming importance 5. Use DSBS to find partners

Preparation: - [ ] Prepare BD lifecycle visual - [ ] Have pipeline stage definitions ready - [ ] Test DSBS access - [ ] Prepare teaming structure comparison

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
BD vs. Capture vs. Proposal 15 min Work done early wins contracts
Pipeline management 10 min Stages help prioritize resources
Gate reviews 10 min Formal go/no-go prevents wasted effort
Teaming 10 min Most wins are team wins

Applied Exercise: BD Process Map + DSBS Research - Map company’s BD process (or create ideal) - Define gate criteria - Search DSBS for 3 potential partners

Common Questions: - “When does capture start?” → When opportunity is qualified and worth resources - “What’s a healthy pipeline size?” → 3-5x annual revenue - “Prime or sub—which is better?” → Depends on capability, risk tolerance, set-aside

Deliverable Check: BD Process Map completed

Week 3, Session 6: Module 3.2 - Competitive Positioning

Learning Objectives: 1. Research competitors using SAM.gov Contract Data 2. Analyze incumbent positioning 3. Develop win themes and discriminators 4. Assess competitive position objectively 5. Document in competitive brief

Preparation: - [ ] Prepare competitor research example - [ ] Have win theme formula ready - [ ] Prepare discriminator examples - [ ] Have ghosting examples ready

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
Competitor research 10 min SAM.gov Contract Data tells you who wins what
Incumbent analysis 10 min Find vulnerabilities, not just strengths
Win themes 15 min Specific, relevant, provable, memorable
Discriminators 10 min What can you say that they can’t?

Applied Exercise: Competitive Brief - Research incumbent on opportunity - Identify 2-3 competitors - Develop 3 win themes - Complete competitive matrix

Common Questions: - “How do I find competitor weaknesses?” → Customer conversations, contract modifications, protests - “Can I mention competitors in my proposal?” → Never by name; ghost instead - “What if I’m the underdog?” → Emphasize innovation, fresh perspective, hunger to perform

Deliverable Check: Competitive Brief completed

Week 4, Session 7: Module 4.1 - Proposal Support Basics

Learning Objectives: 1. Explain proposal development process 2. Read and interpret RFP instructions (Section L/M) 3. Build compliance matrix 4. Identify proposal volumes and purposes 5. Contribute effectively to proposal efforts

Preparation: - [ ] Download sample RFP for exercise - [ ] Prepare Section L/M examples - [ ] Have compliance matrix template ready - [ ] Prepare proposal timeline visual

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
Proposal process 10 min Structured phases; reviews are critical
Section L & M 15 min L = how to submit; M = how you’re scored
Compliance matrix 10 min Miss nothing; map every requirement
Supporting proposals 10 min Everyone has a role; data calls are common

Applied Exercise: Build Compliance Matrix - Download actual RFP from SAM.gov - Extract Section L requirements - Map to proposal sections - Identify at least 15 requirements

Common Questions: - “What happens if I exceed page limits?” → Risk of pages ignored or disqualification - “What’s a Pink Team vs. Red Team?” → Pink = early/outline; Red = near-final - “Do I need to be a writer to help?” → No; subject matter input, reviews, data calls

Deliverable Check: Compliance Matrix (15+ requirements) completed

Week 4, Session 8: Module 4.2 - Contracting Readiness

Learning Objectives: 1. Identify key registrations and certifications 2. Explain basic FAR compliance requirements 3. Describe small business certification programs 4. Recognize internal handoff processes 5. Understand growth metrics

Preparation: - [ ] Prepare registration checklist - [ ] Have certification comparison ready - [ ] Prepare handoff document examples - [ ] Have metrics dashboard example

Key Teaching Points:

Topic Time Key Message
Registrations 10 min SAM.gov is mandatory; keep it current
Certifications 10 min 8(a), HUBZone, etc. open doors
FAR basics 10 min Know contract types; understand compliance
Metrics 15 min Pipeline value, win rate, activity tracking

Applied Exercise: Contracting Readiness Checklist - Verify SAM.gov status - Document certifications - Identify gaps - Review metrics tracking

Program Wrap-Up (additional 15 min): - Review all 8 modules - Discuss next steps - Collect feedback - Present completion certificates

Deliverable Check: Contracting Readiness Checklist completed

Facilitation Tips

Engagement Techniques

Technique When to Use How
Cold Call Re-engage attention “Sarah, what’s your take on…?”
Think-Pair-Share Complex questions 1 min think, 2 min partner, share out
Polling Check understanding “Raise hands if you’ve used SAM.gov Contract Data before”
Case Discussion Apply concepts Present scenario, discuss options
Live Demo Tool navigation Share screen, walk through steps

Handling Different Learner Types

Type Signs Strategy
Expert Knows a lot; may dominate Channel expertise; ask them to help others
Skeptic Questions value Acknowledge experience; focus on practical application
Quiet Doesn’t participate Direct questions; smaller group exercises
Distracted Multitasking Break into activities; make it interactive
Struggling Lost on concepts Pair with stronger peer; offer office hours

Managing Time

  • Running behind: Cut Q&A; assign exercise as homework
  • Running ahead: Extend exercise; add discussion questions
  • Technical issues: Have backup plan; screenshots of tool if system down
