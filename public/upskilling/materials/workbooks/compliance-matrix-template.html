Completion Checklist

Tips for Effective Compliance Matrix

  1. Read the solicitation carefully - Every “shall” and “must” is a requirement
  2. Use solicitation language - Mirror their terms in your responses
  3. Cross-reference - Link each requirement to a specific proposal section
  4. Track owners - Assign responsibility for each section
  5. Version control - Update as amendments are issued
  6. Quality check - Have someone else verify completeness
