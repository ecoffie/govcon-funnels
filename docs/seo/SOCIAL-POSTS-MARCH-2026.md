### LinkedIn Post 1: CAGE Code Guide
**Target:** Business owners starting in GovCon

---

**What's a CAGE Code and why do you need one?**

If you're getting into federal contracting, you'll see "CAGE Code" everywhere.

Here's the 60-second version:

- It's a 5-character code that identifies your company to the government
- You get one FREE when you register on SAM.gov
- Without it, you can't bid on or win federal contracts

The confusion? People think you have to apply for it separately. You don't.

Complete your SAM.gov registration → CAGE code assigned automatically (3-5 days)

We just published a complete guide covering:
- What CAGE codes are used for
- How to look up any company's CAGE code
- Common problems and how to fix them
- The difference between CAGE and UEI

Free guide: https://govcongiants.org/guides/cage-code

### LinkedIn Post 2: Glossary/Terminology
**Target:** New GovCon contractors overwhelmed by acronyms

---

**GovCon has 200+ acronyms. Here are the 10 you actually need to know:**

1. **SAM** - System for Award Management (required registration)
2. **NAICS** - Industry classification codes
3. **CAGE** - Your company ID number
4. **UEI** - Unique Entity Identifier (replaced DUNS)
5. **RFP** - Request for Proposal (the actual bid)
6. **RFI** - Request for Information (market research)
7. **CO** - Contracting Officer (signs contracts)
8. **IDIQ** - Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (contract vehicle)
9. **OSDBU** - Small business office at each agency
10. **APEX** - Free consulting centers (find yours at apexaccelerators.us)

Want the full list? We built a free glossary with 45+ terms:

https://govcongiants.org/glossary
