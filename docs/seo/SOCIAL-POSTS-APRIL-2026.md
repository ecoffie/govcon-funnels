### LinkedIn Post 1: VOSB/SDVOSB Certification
**Target:** Veteran business owners, veteran forums
**Cross-post to:** VETBIZ LinkedIn groups, American Legion Business Network, VetFran

---

**Veteran-owned business? Here's what you need to know about VOSB & SDVOSB certification:**

The VA certifies two types of veteran businesses:

**VOSB (Veteran-Owned Small Business)**
- 51%+ owned by veterans
- Controlled by veterans in daily operations
- Gets VA "Veterans First" contracting priority

**SDVOSB (Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned)**
- Same as VOSB, PLUS
- Owner has service-connected disability rating
- Access to sole-source contracts up to $4.5M (services)
- Even stronger VA preference

The key change: SBA now handles VOSB/SDVOSB certification (as of January 2023).

Apply at: certify.sba.gov

Processing time: 60-90 days on average

- Step-by-step certification process
- Required documentation
- Common rejection reasons and how to avoid them
- VA vs SBA certification differences

Free guide: https://govcongiants.org/guides/vosb-certification

Veterans earned this preference. Make sure you're using it.

### LinkedIn Post 2: HUBZone Certification
**Target:** Businesses in economically disadvantaged areas
**Cross-post to:** Economic development organizations, APEX accelerators, state small business offices

---

**Your zip code could be worth millions in government contracts.**

HUBZone (Historically Underutilized Business Zone) certification gives you:

✅ Set-aside contracts only HUBZone firms can bid on
✅ Sole-source contracts up to $4.5M (services) / $8M (manufacturing)
✅ 10% price evaluation preference on full & open competitions

The requirements:
1. Principal office in a HUBZone
2. At least 35% of employees live in a HUBZone
3. 51%+ owned by US citizens

Check if your area qualifies: maps.certify.sba.gov

Why most businesses miss this:
- They don't know their zip code is in a HUBZone
- They think it's too complicated (it's not)
- They don't realize how valuable the preference is

We wrote a free guide covering:
- How to check HUBZone eligibility
- Employee residence requirements
- Step-by-step certification process
- Maintaining compliance after certification

Free guide: https://govcongiants.org/guides/hubzone-certification

### LinkedIn Post 3: 8(a) Business Development Program
**Target:** Socially/economically disadvantaged business owners
**Cross-post to:** Minority business groups, MBDA centers, SBA district offices

---

**The 8(a) program is the most powerful small business certification. Here's why:**

What you get:
- Sole-source contracts up to $4.5M (services) / $8M (manufacturing)
- 9-year business development period
- SBA mentor-protégé program access
- Joint venture opportunities with large contractors
- Set-aside contracts reserved for 8(a) firms

Who qualifies:
- Socially disadvantaged (rebuttable presumption for certain groups)
- Economically disadvantaged (net worth under $850K excluding home/business)
- 51%+ owned and controlled by qualifying individuals
- Business operational for 2+ years
- "Potential for success" based on financials

The trade-off: It's competitive. Not everyone gets in.

But if you qualify, the 8(a) program can transform your business.

One 8(a) firm we know went from $500K to $15M revenue in 4 years — almost entirely through 8(a) set-asides.

We wrote a complete guide:
- Full eligibility requirements
- Application process step-by-step
- Documentation checklist
- What happens after you're certified

Free guide: https://govcongiants.org/guides/8a-certification

### LinkedIn Post 4: WOSB/EDWOSB Certification
**Target:** Women-owned businesses
**Cross-post to:** NAWBO, women business groups, SBA Women's Business Centers

---

**Women-owned business? You have federal contract opportunities reserved just for you.**

Two certification levels:

**WOSB (Women-Owned Small Business)**
- 51%+ owned by women who are US citizens
- Controlled by women in daily operations
- Set-asides in underrepresented NAICS codes

**EDWOSB (Economically Disadvantaged WOSB)**
- Same as WOSB, PLUS
- Meet economic disadvantage thresholds
- Access to MORE set-aside NAICS codes

Key facts:
- 5% federal contracting goal for WOSBs
- Self-certification is gone — must be SBA-certified
- Apply at certify.sba.gov
- Processing: 60-90 days

The industries with the most WOSB set-asides:
- Professional services (NAICS 541xxx)
- Administrative services (NAICS 561xxx)
- Healthcare (NAICS 621xxx)
- Construction support (NAICS 561xxx)

We wrote a complete guide covering:
- Certification requirements
- Which NAICS codes have WOSB/EDWOSB set-asides
- Application process
- How to find WOSB opportunities

Free guide: https://govcongiants.org/guides/wosb-certification
