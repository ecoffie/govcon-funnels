The jobs board was built without keyword research ("build it and hope they come"). This analysis reveals **35,580 monthly searches** for GovCon job keywords, with several **high-opportunity gaps** that can be addressed immediately.

**Current State:**

**Major Gaps:**

- **"remote government contractor jobs"** (800/mo combined) - No remote jobs page
