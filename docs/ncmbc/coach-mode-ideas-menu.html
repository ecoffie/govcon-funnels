The funder angle is the wedge. Every center said the same thing: they can deliver help all day, but they can't prove it to the people who fund them. Whoever solves the reporting story becomes indispensable — this is the feature most likely to unlock budget. Worth asking NCMBC what her funder actually requires in a report.
