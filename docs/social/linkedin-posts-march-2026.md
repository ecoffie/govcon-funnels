## Post 1: CAGE Code Guide

**Hook:** Authority + Curiosity

---

9,900 people search "CAGE code" every month.

Most find outdated info or pay $500 to companies that do something free.

Here's everything you actually need to know:

**What it is:**
A 5-character ID assigned by the Defense Logistics Agency. Think of it as your government contracting business license number.

**How to get one:**
Register on SAM.gov → Get it automatically → Free
That's it. Don't pay anyone.

**How long it takes:**
7-10 business days (up to 3-5 weeks if validation issues)

**Does it expire?**
Post-2016 codes: Yes, 5-year expiration
Pre-2016 codes: No, but goes inactive if SAM lapses
Solution: Renew SAM.gov annually

**How to look one up:**
→ SAM.gov entity search (fastest)
→ cage.dla.mil (DLA direct)
→ No account needed for basic searches

I put the complete guide together with step-by-step instructions, troubleshooting, and FAQs.

## Post 2: GovCon Glossary

**Hook:** Pattern + Value

---

I analyzed 500+ questions from new government contractors.

The same 45 terms kept coming up.

So I built a free glossary that explains each one in plain English.

Here are 10 terms every contractor needs to know:

1. **CAGE Code** - Your 5-character federal business ID
2. **UEI** - Replaced DUNS number in 2022 (12 characters)
3. **NAICS Code** - Tells agencies what you sell
4. **Set-Aside** - Contract reserved for small/disadvantaged biz
5. **IDIQ** - Indefinite quantity contract (task orders)
6. **RFP** - Request for Proposal (formal bid request)
7. **RFI** - Request for Information (market research)
8. **Sources Sought** - Agency asking "who can do this?"
9. **FAR** - Federal Acquisition Regulation (the rules)
10. **Past Performance** - Your track record on prior contracts

The glossary covers all 45+ terms with definitions, related guides, and real examples.
