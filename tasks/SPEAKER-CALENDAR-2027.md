## The audience weighting — decided by the business, not preference

Eric didn't pick a lane, so this is weighted on what the company's own record says:

| Evidence | Where |

| **APEX Accelerators documented as a distribution channel** — 90+ locations, 300+ counselors, 30,000+ small businesses served annually. *"Their clients = our target customer."* | `market-assassin/docs/strategy/APEX-GROWTH-STRATEGY.md` |

| The strongest research asset — the PSNS single-offer finding — is an **acquisition** story, not a contractor one | `tasks/PRD-supplier-activation.md` |

**Weighting: advisor networks first, government/acquisition second, contractors direct last.**

One talk to ~1,000 SBDC advisors or 300 APEX counselors reaches more contractors than any
direct-to-contractor stage, and it maps onto a product tier that already exists. The government
track is second because it feeds the Institute and the buyer-side product, and it's where the
PSNS evidence is genuinely differentiated. Direct-to-contractor is last — most crowded field,
weakest fit for a research thesis.

## Cadence: 8–12 events, not 50

Eric's original ask was "gigs each week." That's ~50 events/year — a full-time circuit that
consumes exactly the research time that makes the pitch different. The SAME session works because
it's *"we measured this,"* and that measurement doesn't happen from a plane.

**Target: one flagship per quarter + webinars in between.** A webinar costs a day instead of a
week, and — as both 2026 emails argue — a successful webinar is the strongest possible case for a
main-stage slot the following year. That's the pipeline, not a consolation prize.

## The platform — one thesis, two expressions

**Discovery Is the New Competitive Advantage** — *discovery beats search.*

- **Advisors / small business:** *Stop Searching for Contracts: Discover Your Federal Market*
  — the framework: Capability → Market → Buyers → Competitors → Opportunities → Decision
- **Acquisition / government:** *Beyond Posting: How Market Intelligence Can Expand Competition*
  — grounded in the PSNS finding: a $12.7M set-aside, one offer, ~100 relevant suppliers, 4 in the
  sampled award record

Same research, two sides of the market. Speaking, product, and the Institute reinforce one idea
rather than twenty separate talks.

**Both 2026 programs made AI a named priority** — SBDC runs an "AI U Advisor Training" track and a
general session on AI growth; SAME's "Spurring Innovation and Technology" track explicitly covers
AI integration **and risk management**. The risk half is the differentiated angle: an AI tool that
reports "100 capable firms" without saying it measured registration rather than capability is
handing a contracting officer a number they shouldn't rely on.
