# GovCon Giants SaaS SEO Strategy

**Created:** May 9, 2026
**Status:** Active
**Goal:** Position govcongiants.com as the affordable alternative to GovWin/GovTribe for small business federal contractors

---

## Executive Summary

The government contracting software market is dominated by expensive enterprise solutions (GovWin $13K-$119K/year, GovTribe $1,350-$5,500/year). There's a massive opportunity to capture small business contractors searching for affordable alternatives.

**Our Positioning:** "80% of GovWin capability at 2% of the cost" - $149/month vs $25K/year

---

## Competitive Landscape

### Enterprise Tier ($10K+/year)
| Competitor | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **GovWin IQ** | $13K-$119K/year | 150+ analysts, deep pre-RFP intel | Expensive, enterprise-focused |
| **Bloomberg Government** | $7,500-$14K/year | Policy + legislative intel | Not BD-focused |
| **GovSpend** | $7,300-$24,500/year | SLED data, discretionary spending | Federal limited |

### Mid-Market Tier ($1K-$5K/year)
| Competitor | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **GovTribe** | $1,350-$5,500/year | Transparent pricing, SAM.gov focus | Limited SLED |
| **Federal Compass** | $0-$15K/user/year | Capture-focused, competitive intel | Complex pricing |
| **EZGovOpps** | $2,700-$6,000/year | IDIQ/program intel, file cabinet | Dated interface |
| **HigherGov** | $500-$2,500/year | Data pages (SEO), CRM | Limited analysis |

### Budget Tier ($0-$500/year)
| Competitor | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Fed-Spend** | $49-$199/month | AI-powered, affordable | Newer, less data |
| **FedScout** | Free-$99/month | Free search, win probability | Limited intel |
| **SAM.gov** | Free | Official source, complete data | No analysis, clunky UI |

### AI-Powered Newcomers (2023-2026)
| Competitor | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Sweetspot** | Custom | Y Combinator, AI proposals | No public pricing |
| **GovDash** | Custom | AI BD enablement, 50% faster proposals | Enterprise-focused |
| **Procura Federal** | $399/month | AI scoring, attachment reading | Federal only |
| **SamSearch** | Custom | Natural language AI search | No public pricing |
| **CLEATUS** | Custom | Full lifecycle AI | Very new |

### Schema Markup

1. Homepage → Features → Pricing → Compare pages

4. Blog posts → Comparison pages where relevant
5. All pages → Pricing (footer + CTAs)
